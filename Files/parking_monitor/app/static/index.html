Architecture & Engineering of the Smart Parking Monitor

Discover how convolutional neural networks, Bayesian Kalman tracking, 2D/3D spatial geometry, and real-time hardware acceleration operate in synchronization.

Module 1 • Ingestion & Stream
📡 Decoupled Asynchronous Architecture

To prevent server bottlenecks and maintain minimal CPU usage, video acquisition operates on an independent secondary worker thread.

  • Intelligent Frame Pacing: The capture loop regulates the refresh rate to ~25-30 FPS with active sleep intervals between frames.
  • Async HTTP Streaming: Live video is dispatched via FastAPI async generators in multipart/x-mixed-replace format without blocking the REST API.
  • INTER_AREA HD Sampling: Crisp scaling to 720p without aliasing or blur, preserving license plates and painted demarcation lines.
Module 2 • Neural Network
🎯 Vehicle Detection with YOLOv8

Computer vision utilizes deep neural networks trained on perimeter surveillance, processing 640x640 tensors in low-latency milliseconds.

  • Strict Multi-Class Filtering: Exclusively isolates cars, motorcycles, buses, and trucks, filtering out pedestrians, shopping carts, and static clutter.
  • Balanced Confidence Threshold (0.22): Calibrated to capture distant vehicles, shadowed parking bays, or intense asphalt sun glare.
  • Non-Maximum Suppression (NMS): Merges overlapping bounding proposals into high-precision spatial bounding boxes.
Module 3 • Tracking
🔄 Persistent Tracking with ByteTrack

In a parking facility, vehicles maneuver, halt, and encounter transient occlusions from pillars, trees, or pedestrians.

  • Kalman Filtering: Models the state vector (position, velocity, aspect ratio) to predict trajectory across frames.
  • Two-Stage Association: Pairs high-confidence detections first, then rescues low-confidence proposals (partially occluded vehicles).
  • Persistent Identity (#ID): Retains a consistent numeric identifier from vehicular entry until exit.
Module 4 • Perspective & Space
📐 3D Geometry & Ground Footprint

With tilted CCTV cameras, the roofline of a car in Bay 2 visually overlaps into Bay 1. Our algorithm solves this parallax distortion:

  • Ground Footprint Isolation: Discards the top 52% of the bounding box (floating roof and glass) to evaluate only the tire contact patch on asphalt.
  • Axle Anchor Verification: Confirms the physical center of the wheels falls within the calibrated Shapely vector polygon.
  • Exclusive 1-to-1 Assignment: A vehicle can never occupy two bays simultaneously; the bay with the highest ground overlap claims exclusive occupancy.
Module 5 • Performance
🚫 Exclusion Zones (Pre-Masking)

Allows defining regions where the model should omit all computation (such as external public roadways, pedestrian sidewalks, or off-property areas).

  • Pixel Zeroing: The excluded region is filled with zeros on the internal inference tensor, preventing redundant candidate proposals.
  • Up to 300% Acceleration: Bypassing street traffic tracking reduces frame processing latency from 78ms to 26ms.
  • Pristine Visual Feed: On the Live Monitor, the video stream remains 100% natural, without artificial black boxes or blemishes.
Module 6 • Business Logic
⏱️ Hysteresis (Debounce) & Database

A vehicle driving past an aisle must not mark a vacant parking bay as occupied for a transient fraction of a second.

  • Confirmation Window (4 frames): Requires continuous presence to confirm arrival, and sustained absence to confirm departure.
  • SQLite Session Logging: On confirmed arrival, the session is logged with an ISO timestamp and activates the real-time dwell stopwatch.
  • Audit & CSV Export: On departure, the total elapsed dwell time is finalized for billing, audits, or analysis in Power BI and Excel.
Module 7 • Multi-GPU Hardware
⚡ DirectML & CUDA Acceleration

The system automatically detects physical host GPUs at boot to compile the computation graph on the optimal acceleration engine:

  • AMD Radeon RX (DirectML): ONNX Runtime execution provider over DirectX 12 with native support for RDNA 2/3 hardware.
  • NVIDIA GeForce / RTX (CUDA): Massive parallel acceleration across Tensor and CUDA cores for ONNX and PyTorch weights.
  • Anti Spin-Wait Policy: Configures OMP_WAIT_POLICY=PASSIVE and KMP_BLOCKTIME=0 to eliminate idle CPU spin-locks.
Module 8 • Analytics
📊 Key Metrics & Turnover (KPIs)

The dashboard centralizes operational intelligence in real time for efficient parking facility management:

  • Occupancy Rate: Dynamic percentage with a chromatic progress bar (emerald / amber / scarlet).
  • Average Dwell Time: Continuous weighted duration monitoring of currently parked vehicles.
  • Cumulative Daily Traffic: Real-time turnover counters and total vehicle throughput across operating hours.
