SQL Queries - Northwind Database

Data analysis and visualizations for decision making

  • Dataset: Northwind (sample sales company)
  • Main tables: Products, Orders, Customers, Employees
  • Technologies: SQLite, Python, pandas, matplotlib, plotly

Project Context

Analysis of the Northwind database, a classic dataset that simulates a sales company (products, orders, customers and employees). Using SQL queries and Python visualizations, we explore sales patterns, employee performance, table relationships and key metrics for business decisions.

Database Structure:

  • EMPLOYEE: Employee information
  • CATEGORY: Product categories
  • CUSTOMER: Customer data
  • SHIPPER: Shipping companies
  • SUPPLIER: Product suppliers
  • ORDER: Orders placed
  • PRODUCT: Available products
  • ORDERDETAIL: Order line items
  • REGION/TERRITORY: Geographic regions

Main Queries Executed

1. Top-selling Products (by quantity)

SELECT p.ProductName, SUM(od.Quantity) AS TotalSold
FROM OrderDetail od
JOIN Product p ON od.ProductId = p.Id
GROUP BY p.ProductName
ORDER BY TotalSold DESC
LIMIT 10;

This query joins order details with products to sum how many units have been sold per product. It groups by product name and orders to show the top sellers.


2. Top Customers by Total Spend

SELECT c.CompanyName, SUM(od.UnitPrice * od.Quantity) AS TotalSpent
FROM 'Order' o
JOIN Customer c ON o.CustomerId = c.Id
JOIN OrderDetail od ON o.Id = od.OrderId
GROUP BY c.CompanyName
ORDER BY TotalSpent DESC
LIMIT 10;

Joins orders, customers and order details to calculate how much each customer has spent (price × quantity). It groups by company and shows the top spenders.


3. Monthly Sales (grouped by year-month)

SELECT strftime('%Y-%m', o.OrderDate) AS Month,
    SUM(od.UnitPrice * od.Quantity) AS TotalSales
FROM 'Order' o
JOIN OrderDetail od ON o.Id = od.OrderId
    GROUP BY Month
ORDER BY Month;

Calculates monthly sales by joining orders and their details, grouping by year-month. Useful to analyze sales trends.


4. Employee with Highest Sales Generated

SELECT e.FirstName || ' ' || e.LastName AS Employee,
    SUM(od.UnitPrice * od.Quantity) AS SalesGenerated
FROM 'Order' o
JOIN Employee e ON o.EmployeeId = e.Id
JOIN OrderDetail od ON o.Id = od.OrderId
    GROUP BY Employee
ORDER BY SalesGenerated DESC
 LIMIT 1;

Relates employees to the orders they handled and sums the value sold. Returns the employee with the highest sales generated.


5. Low-stock Products (reorder alert)

SELECT ProductName, UnitsInStock, ReorderLevel
FROM Product
WHERE UnitsInStock < ReorderLevel
ORDER BY UnitsInStock ASC;

Shows products whose current stock is below the reorder level. Useful to identify items that need replenishment.


6. Top Categories by Revenue

SELECT c.CategoryName, SUM(od.UnitPrice * od.Quantity) AS SalesByCategory
FROM OrderDetail od
JOIN Product p ON od.ProductId = p.Id
JOIN Category c ON p.CategoryId = c.Id
GROUP BY c.CategoryName
ORDER BY SalesByCategory DESC
LIMIT 5;

Joins order details, products and categories to sum revenue by category. Shows the top categories.


7. Slowest Suppliers (average delivery time)

SELECT s.CompanyName AS Supplier,
    ROUND(AVG(julianday(o.ShippedDate) - julianday(o.OrderDate)), 2) AS AvgDays
FROM 'Order' o
JOIN OrderDetail od ON o.Id = od.OrderId
JOIN Product p ON od.ProductId = p.Id
JOIN Supplier s ON p.SupplierId = s.Id
WHERE o.ShippedDate IS NOT NULL
GROUP BY Supplier
ORDER BY AvgDays DESC;

Computes the average time between order date and shipped date per supplier. Useful to detect delivery delays.


8. Inactive Customers (no orders in over 1 year)

SELECT c.CompanyName AS Customer,
    MAX(o.OrderDate) AS LastOrder
FROM Customer c
LEFT JOIN 'Order' o ON c.Id = o.CustomerId
GROUP BY c.Id
HAVING UltimoPedido < date('now', '-1 year') OR UltimoPedido IS NULL
ORDER BY UltimoPedido;

Uses LEFT JOIN to include customers without orders. Filters those who haven't purchased in more than one year or never purchased.


9. Sales vs Stock (inventory analysis)

SELECT p.ProductName,
    SUM(od.Quantity) AS UnitsSold,
    p.UnitsInStock AS CurrentStock,
    (p.UnitsInStock - SUM(od.Quantity)) AS Difference
FROM OrderDetail od
JOIN Product p ON od.ProductId = p.Id
GROUP BY p.Id
ORDER BY UnitsSold DESC;

Compares available stock with units sold per product. Useful to detect shortages or excess inventory.


10. Customers and Purchase Frequency

SELECT c.CompanyName AS Customer,
    COUNT(o.Id) AS TotalOrders,
    SUM(od.UnitPrice * od.Quantity) AS TotalAmount
FROM Customer c
LEFT JOIN 'Order' o ON c.Id = o.CustomerId
LEFT JOIN OrderDetail od ON o.Id = od.OrderId
    GROUP BY c.Id
ORDER BY TotalOrders DESC;

Lists customers with the number of orders placed and total amount spent. Uses LEFT JOIN to include customers without purchases.
